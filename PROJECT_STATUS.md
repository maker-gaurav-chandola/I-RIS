## Current Scope

I-RIS is primarily an interview practice and improvement platform. Its core purpose is to help students and freshers practice realistic technical interviews through adaptive conversations, contextual follow-up questions, voice interaction, and structured performance feedback.

I-RIS is not an actual recruitment platform, job portal, hiring system, resume builder, coding platform, personality detector, facial emotion detector, or candidate hiring recommendation system.
